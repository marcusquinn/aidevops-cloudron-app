# AI DevOps Worker visual assets

The Cloudron package uses the established AI DevOps terminal mark and dark
cyan brand palette. It does not introduce a separate product identity.

## Publishing assets

- `logo.png` is the 256×256 Cloudron icon derived from the official AI DevOps
  touch icon.
- `media/hero.png` is a 1200×400 crop of the official AI DevOps social image,
  preserving the logo, product name, value proposition, and headline metrics.
- Keep screenshots and heroes free of credentials, repository names, local
  paths, worker prompts, and customer data.
- Cloudron listing media should remain public over HTTPS and use a 3:1 aspect
  ratio. Re-check readability after any source-brand update.

These files are distribution metadata only; they do not change the worker
dashboard interface.
